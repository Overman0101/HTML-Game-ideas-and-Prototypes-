Thor: Storm Simulator

Hurl Mjolnir, call it home, and bring the lightning down on the draugr crawling out of the pines.

WASD
Move. Hold Shift to sprint, Space to jump
Left clickF
Tap to throw Mjolnir, or hold to spin it and throw harder. With no hammer in hand, punch
Right clickV
Swing Mjolnir, alternating an overhead slam and a sweep
RRight click
Recall Mjolnir. Right click recalls when your hand is empty. Stand next to it and press R to lift it out of the ground
Space
Jump. Double-tap to fly, or tap while spinning Mjolnir. While flying, tap to land
Z
Hold to fire a lightning beam from Mjolnir, or from your open hands when it's away
E
Call lightning down at the crosshair
Q
Call lightning onto Mjolnir to charge it
M
Mute sound

Mjolnir ricochets between draugr, lands head first, and blasts the ground around it. Spin it for about a second to reach full power. In flight, steer with the mouse, W to speed up, S to slow, Shift to boost, A and D to bank. If your browser blocks mouse capture, use the arrow keys to look around, or open the file in its own tab.
